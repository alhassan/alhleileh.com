Al Hleileh

Al Hleileh is the Co-Founder and CEO of Civic Marketplace, a company rebuilding the way local government buys: a transparent marketplace where public sector buyers can discover, evaluate and procure from a much wider pool of suppliers.

In 2026 Al was an expert speaker at the Snohomish County Civic Assembly on AI in Washington State, the largest US local government to host a civic assembly and the first to use one for AI policy. Over three weekends, 40 randomly selected residents heard from experts, then developed recommendations for how the county should use AI.

Previously, Al led technology and data analytics at Delivery Associates, where his work on digital transformation and data-driven decision-making helped governments improve performance. He also shaped product and analytics strategy at Spring.

Al holds an MBA from Stanford and the NIGP Public Procurement Supplier Masters designation, and has served on the NIGP Business Council since April 2026. He is based in London. He spends a lot of time on the road in the USA.
